Access control

Security first: not everybody sees everything.

Internal admin planning view. This page should not be visible to public visitors, subscribers, clients, or talent. On Vercel, this should be enforced with authenticated API routes, role checks, database row ownership, and server-side validation.

Roles

  • Owner/Admin: full access to clients, talent, payments, reports, and settings.
  • Operations Manager: projects, onboarding, talent matching, tasks, and KPIs.
  • Sales/Marketing: leads, campaigns, service requests, and prospect notes.
  • Finance: subscriptions, invoices, refunds, payment status, and billing notes.
  • Company Admin: business owner who manages company users, requests, billing summaries, and upgrades.
  • Client: only their company profile, requests, assigned project, invoices, and reports.
  • Talent: only their profile, training, opportunities, assigned tasks, and messages.

Protected pages

  • Admin dashboard: owner and internal team only.
  • Client portal: logged-in clients only, filtered by their company ID.
  • Talent portal: logged-in talent only, filtered by member ID.
  • Payment/subscription pages: authenticated customers only.
  • Competitor map and AI automation rules: internal or owner-only.
  • Public pages: Home, Services, Pricing, About, Contact, Apply.

Collection rules

  • Clients: admin write, client read only for owned company records.
  • Talent: admin write, talent read/update limited public profile fields.
  • Projects: admin write, client/talent read only assigned records.
  • Payments: owner/admin only; never expose full payment details in custom code.
  • Messages/tasks: member-scoped queries through backend logic.
  • AuditLog: append-only from backend, readable by owner/admin only.

Backend controls

  • Validate all form input before inserting into CMS collections.
  • Use backend web modules for sensitive queries and matching logic.
  • Use data hooks to normalize emails, reject bad statuses, and stamp owner IDs.
  • Keep secrets in Vercel environment variables, never frontend code or public data.
  • Log admin actions for client, talent, subscription, and project changes.

Agentic AI guardrails

  • AI can draft matches, task plans, and KPI summaries, but admin approves before records change.
  • AI tools receive filtered records only; no full payment data or private documents.
  • Every AI recommendation stores source records, confidence, reviewer, and approval status.
  • AI automations run from backend functions, never public page code.
  • Owner can pause AI automations from a protected settings page.

Role-based views

  • Subscriber sees resources and their own service requests.
  • Client sees their projects, KPIs, tasks, invoices, and assigned talent only.
  • Talent sees training, availability, assigned work, and approved opportunities only.
  • Internal team sees operational dashboards based on assigned department.
  • Owner sees system settings, roles, audit logs, and strategic research.
